03 · How I work

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Map the process

    I learn how the work happens today before touching a node, and point out where automation won't pay off.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    You approve a written scope with clear milestones before I build, so there are no surprises on price or timeline.

  3. 03

    Build and harden

    Every system ships with retries, error handling, cost caps and monitoring. If something breaks, you get an alert.

  4. 04

    Hand it over

    Plain-language documentation and a handover video, so your team owns it and you're never locked in.

Safe by default

Webhooks verified with shared secrets and HMAC signatures. Agents never claim a booking that didn't happen.

You approve what goes out

Outreach emails, posts and proposals wait for you to press Approve before anything is sent.

No AI where it isn't needed

When a flow has to be auditable, like a booking, I build it as a plain state machine and keep the LLM out of it.
